## Getting Started

### What is the User Role?

As a User, you are part of an Admin's group. You can:
- Receive USD transfers from your Admin
- Exchange USD to SYP or TRY
- Create and manage your personal expenses
- View your own financial transactions
- Export your personal financial reports

### Navigation Overview

The User app has five main sections:
- **Home**: View your balances and incoming transfers
- **Exchange**: Convert USD to SYP or TRY
- **Expenses**: Create and view your expenses with invoices
- **Export**: Generate PDF and Excel reports
- **Profile**: Manage your account settings

## Registration and Login

### First-Time Registration

To register as a User, you need a 6-character group code from your Admin.

1. Open the User app
2. Tap "Register" on the welcome screen
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - **Admin Join Code** (6 characters)
4. Tap "Register"

### Getting Your Admin Join Code

Contact your Admin to get the 6-character code needed for registration.

### Logging In

1. Open the User app
2. Enter your email and password
3. Tap "Login"

## Home (Financial Box)

### Viewing Your Balances

The Home page displays your current balances in three currencies:
- USD (United States Dollar)

Each balance shows:

### Understanding Your Balance Sources

Your balances come from:
- **Incoming**: USD transfers from your Admin
- **Exchanges**: Converting USD to SYP or TRY

### Viewing Incoming Transfers

Below your balance card, you'll see all incoming transfers from your Admin:
- Transfer amount (USD)

- Status

### Refreshing Your Data

Pull down on the Home page to refresh your balances and transfer history.

## Currency Exchange

### Why Exchange Currency?

You receive USD from your Admin but may need SYP or TRY for local expenses. The Exchange feature lets you convert USD to these currencies.

### Creating an Exchange

1. Navigate to Exchange page
2. Fill in the exchange details:
   - **Target Currency**: Select SYP or TRY
   - **Amount in USD**: Enter how much USD to exchange
   - **Exchange Rate**: Enter the current rate
   - **OR Converted Amount**: Enter the final amount (system calculates rate)
   - **Date**: Select exchange date
   - **Notes**: Add optional notes (optional)
3. Review the calculation
4. Tap "Create Exchange"

**Important**: The system will verify you have sufficient USD balance before creating the exchange.

### Understanding Exchange Calculations

You can enter either:
- **Exchange Rate**: System calculates converted amount
- **Converted Amount**: System calculates exchange rate

Example:
- Exchange 100 USD to SYP
- Rate: 13,000 (1 USD = 13,000 SYP)
- Result: 1,300,000 SYP

### Viewing Exchange History

1. Tap "Exchange Log" button at the top of Exchange page
2. View all your exchanges (only yours, not other users')

### Exchange Log Features

The Exchange Log shows:
- Date of exchange
- Amount in USD
- Target currency (SYP or TRY)
- Exchange rate used
- Converted amount
- Notes

### Filtering Exchange Log

1. In Exchange Log, tap the filter icon
2. Select currency filter:
   - **All**: Show both SYP and TRY exchanges
   - **SYP**: Show only Syrian Pound exchanges
   - **TRY**: Show only Turkish Lira exchanges
3. Tap "Apply"

### Exporting Exchange Log

1. Apply desired filters (optional)
2. Tap "Export to PDF"
3. Save or share the PDF file

## Expenses

### Creating an Expense

1. Navigate to Expenses page
2. Tap the "+" or "New Expense" button
3. Fill in the expense details:
   - **Description**: What was purchased
   - **Currency**: Select USD, SYP, or TRY
   - **Amount**: Enter the expense amount
   - **Date**: Select expense date
   - **Invoice Photo**: Tap to add receipt photo (optional)
4. Review the details
5. Tap "Create Expense"

**Important**: The system will verify you have sufficient balance in the selected currency.

### Adding Invoice Photos

1. When creating an expense, tap "Add Invoice Photo"
2. Choose source:
   - **Camera**: Take a photo now
   - **Gallery**: Select existing photo
3. Photo will be compressed and uploaded
4. Thumbnail appears in the expense form

### Viewing Your Expenses

The Expenses page shows all your personal expenses. Each expense card displays:
- Description
- Amount and currency
- Date
- Invoice preview button (if photo exists)

**Note**: You can only see your own expenses, not expenses from other users.

### Previewing Invoice Photos

1. Find an expense with an invoice photo
2. Tap the "Preview" or camera icon
3. Full-screen image opens with zoom controls
4. Tap outside or "Close" to dismiss

### Filtering Expenses

1. Tap the filter icon
2. Select filters:
   - **Date Range**: Select start and end dates
   - **Currency**: Select USD, SYP, or TRY
3. Tap "Apply"

**Note**: Filters persist when you navigate to Export page.

### Clearing Filters

1. Tap the filter icon
2. Tap "Clear All Filters"
3. All your expenses will be displayed

## Export

### Available Export Formats

The Export page offers three export options:
1. **Export to PDF**: Formatted expense report
2. **Export to Excel**: Spreadsheet format
3. **Export Invoice Images to PDF Bundle**: All your invoice photos in one PDF

### Exporting Expenses to PDF

1. Navigate to Export page
2. Ensure desired filters are applied (from Expenses page)
3. Tap "Export to PDF"
4. Wait for export to complete
5. Save or share the PDF file

The PDF includes:
- Your expense list with all details
- Totals by currency
- Date range and filters applied

### Exporting to Excel

1. Navigate to Export page
2. Tap "Export to Excel"
3. Wait for export to complete
4. Save or share the Excel file

The Excel file includes:
- One row per expense
- Columns: Date, Description, Amount, Currency
- Totals at the bottom

### Exporting Invoice Images

1. Navigate to Export page
2. Tap "Export Invoice Images to PDF Bundle"
3. Wait for export to complete (may take longer for many images)
4. Save or share the PDF file

The PDF bundle includes:
- All your invoice photos from filtered expenses
- One photo per page
- Expense details below each photo

### Understanding Filter Persistence

Filters you apply on the Expenses page automatically apply to exports:
- Date range filters
- Currency filters

This ensures your exports match what you see on the Expenses page.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### "Insufficient Balance" Error

**Problem**: Cannot create exchange or expense due to insufficient balance.

**Solution**: 
1. Check your current balance in the required currency
2. For USD: Wait for Admin transfer or reduce amount
3. For SYP/TRY: Exchange more USD to the needed currency
4. Try the operation again

#### No Incoming Transfers Showing

**Problem**: Expected transfer from Admin is not visible.

**Solution**:
1. Pull down to refresh the Home page
2. Check with your Admin that transfer was sent
3. Verify you're in the correct group
4. Logout and login again
5. Contact your Admin if issue persists

#### Cannot Upload Invoice Photo

**Problem**: Photo upload fails or doesn't appear.

**Solution**:
1. Check camera/storage permissions in device settings
2. Ensure photo is not too large (max 10MB)
3. Check internet connection
4. Try taking a new photo or selecting a different one
5. Restart the app and try again

#### Exchange Calculation Wrong

**Problem**: Exchange rate or converted amount doesn't match expectations.

**Solution**:
1. Verify you entered the correct exchange rate
2. Check if you entered rate or converted amount (not both)
3. System calculates: Converted Amount = USD Amount × Exchange Rate
4. Double-check your math
5. Clear the form and start over

#### Export Not Working

**Problem**: Export button doesn't work or file doesn't download.

**Solution**:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure you have storage permissions enabled
3. Try exporting again
4. For invoice images, ensure expenses have photos attached
5. If problem persists, contact support

#### Cannot Join Group

**Problem**: Group code is not working during registration.

**Solution**:
1. Verify you have the correct 6-character code from your Admin
2. Check for typos (codes are case-sensitive)
3. Ensure you're using the User flavor app (not Admin or Superadmin)
4. Ask your Admin if the code was recently regenerated
5. Contact your Admin for a new code

### Performance Issues

#### App Running Slowly

**Solutions**:
- Close and restart the app
- Clear app cache in device settings
- Ensure you have stable internet connection
- Update to the latest app version

#### Data Not Loading

**Solutions**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Pull down to refresh
3. Logout and login again
4. Contact support if issue persists

### Security Best Practices

1. **Use Strong Password**: Minimum 8 characters with mix of letters, numbers, symbols
2. **Regular Password Changes**: Change password every 3-6 months
3. **Logout on Shared Devices**: Always logout when using shared devices
4. **Keep Receipts**: Always attach invoice photos for your records
5. **Regular Backups**: Export your data monthly for backup

## Best Practices

### Financial Management

1. **Regular Balance Checks**: Monitor your balances daily
2. **Timely Exchanges**: Exchange USD when you have good rates
3. **Invoice Photos**: Always attach invoice photos for accountability
4. **Accurate Descriptions**: Use clear expense descriptions
5. **Regular Exports**: Export reports monthly for your records

### Expense Tracking

1. **Immediate Entry**: Record expenses as soon as they happen
2. **Clear Descriptions**: Use descriptive names (e.g., "Office supplies - pens and paper")
3. **Correct Currency**: Always select the currency you actually paid in
4. **Actual Dates**: Use the date you made the purchase, not today's date
5. **Photo Quality**: Take clear, readable photos of receipts

### Data Organization

1. **Use Filters**: Apply filters to find specific transactions
2. **Consistent Dates**: Use actual transaction dates
3. **Add Notes**: Include helpful notes in exchanges
4. **Regular Exports**: Keep backup copies of your reports
